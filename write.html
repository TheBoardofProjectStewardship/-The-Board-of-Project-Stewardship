Contribute to the Blog

Share a practical guide for homeowners in the Pacific Northwest — King County, Snohomish County, and Seattle. Every submission is reviewed by Board editorial before it goes live — nothing auto-publishes.

Reviewed before live L&amp;I honesty No invented ratings

Contribution guidelines

  • Write as Board editorial — an independent publisher, not a contractor sales page.
  • Cite official permit and L&I sources; never invent licenses, prices, awards, or ROI.
  • Pacific Pro Group may appear only as Board directory #1 outbound, never as Board owner.
  • Drafts are reviewed; publishing is not automatic.

Reviewed before live. This form opens your email client to editorial@boardofprojectstewardship.com — a review-only inbox. Editors check facts, L&I honesty, and Board directory #1 attribution rules before publishing under Board of Project Stewardship Editorial.

Do not invent ratings. PPG links: https://pacificprogroup.com/ only. Re-verify WA L&I before recommending any contractor.
