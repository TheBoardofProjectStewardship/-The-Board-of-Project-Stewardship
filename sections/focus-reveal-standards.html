The Board of Project Stewardship helps homeowners in Edmonds and coastal King & Snohomish Counties judge contractors by stewardship standards — not by who buys the loudest ads. We look for financial solvency, a clear project steward, local mastery of coastal permits, an active WA L&I license, and explicit permit ownership. Technical work is read through the McMorrough Protocol — an editorial lens, not a license. Marketing budgets do not move rankings; public records and accountable practice do.
