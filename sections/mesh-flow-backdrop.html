The Board of Project Stewardship Mission
Our mission

Better questions. Better stewardship.

The Board of Project Stewardship exists to help homeowners navigate construction with more clarity. We organize practical standards around verification, accountability, local context, and informed decision-making so people can evaluate projects and contractors with fewer assumptions.

01 · Verify Use current evidence.

Prefer public records, written scope, current documentation, and project-specific facts over unsupported impressions.

02 · Clarify Define responsibility.

Make ownership of permits, coordination, decisions, changes, and unresolved issues clear before construction becomes complicated.

03 · Steward Protect the whole process.

Good stewardship considers not only the finished space, but how the project is planned, documented, communicated, and carried through.

What this means

Construction standards are most useful when they help people see what is known, what is uncertain, and what still needs verification.

The Board of Project Stewardship provides educational guidance and comparative frameworks. It does not replace legal, financial, architectural, engineering, geotechnical, code, permitting, insurance, or other project-specific professional advice, and it does not guarantee contractor performance or construction outcomes.
