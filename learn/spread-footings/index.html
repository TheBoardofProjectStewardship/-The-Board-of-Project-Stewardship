How wide the concrete is

A footing is wider than the wall so the same load presses the soil more gently. This sketch does not size one.

Soil can take only so much pressure. Spreading the load over a wider footing keeps that pressure down. A continuous footing follows a bearing wall. An isolated footing sits under a post. The bottom has to reach the depth the local code sets for frost, unless the design is a frost-protected slab, which is its own assembly.

The highlighted base of this house is the idea of that line. Bearing pressure, reinforcement, and thickness are calculated from the loads and the soil. None of those numbers are in the drawing.
