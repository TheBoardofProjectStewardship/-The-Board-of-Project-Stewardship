How a house is built

Roof framing

Rafters and trusses make the roof shape and tie the walls. This house shows a gable, a hip, and a dormer.

A common rafter runs from the wall to the ridge. A tie or a ceiling joist keeps the two eaves from spreading. A hip rafter runs to an outside corner. A valley rafter sits where two slopes meet in a gutter. A dormer is a small roof and wall cut into a slope, and it has to flash into that slope.

This house has an east gable, a hip toward the balcony, a dormer on the front, and lower roofs on the garage and the porch. Trusses can make the same shapes. The planes are drawn. The members inside them are not.
