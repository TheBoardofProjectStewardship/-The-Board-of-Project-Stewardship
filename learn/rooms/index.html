How a house is built

Rooms on this plan

The rooms drawn on this house: garage and main floor, then bedrooms, baths, and laundry above.

The main floor, as drawn, holds a two-car garage, a utility room, a pantry, the kitchen and its island, dining, a living room with a fireplace, a powder room, a mudroom, the foyer and entrance, a home office, storage, and a patio. The stair leaves this floor near the middle.

The upper floor holds a primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and a bath, two more bedrooms, two baths, laundry, and a balcony beside the garage. The same stair arrives here. The garage roof is below the upper floor, not a room.

Those names are the plan. The sketch does not give furniture clearances, door swings, or a finish schedule. Overall width on the upper sheet is about 45 feet. The garage is about 22 feet. Other small figures on the sheets are too unclear to treat as dimensions.
