How a house is built

Layers of an outside wall

Cladding, a drainage gap, the weather barrier, sheathing, the stud cavity, and the interior board. One wall, several jobs.

From the weather inward, this example shows cladding, a drainage gap, a water-resistive barrier, sheathing, insulated stud cavities, and interior board. The barrier and its flashings form the drainage plane. Sheathing may also brace the wall against sideways load.

Western Washington is marine climate zone 4C. Rain is the main wetting, and winter drying is slow. Whether the interior face uses a vapor retarder, and which class, depends on the whole assembly and the code edition. A sheet of plastic on the inside can trap water if the outside cannot dry. This section is the idea of the layers. It is not a wall specification for this house.
