How a house is built

Cut and fill

How a sloping lot is cut and filled to make a level pad, and why the soil under the house is not topsoil.

On a slope the house wants a level pad. Soil taken from the high side is the cut. Soil placed on the low side is the fill. The volumes are rarely equal once organic soil is stripped and the fill is compacted.

Fill that will carry a footing is placed in layers and compacted. Topsoil, stumps, and debris do not belong in that fill. They settle. A soils report, when the site needs one, says what the ground can carry and whether the fill is allowed under the house.

This model is already a flat pad. It shows the result, not the hillside and not a grading plan.
