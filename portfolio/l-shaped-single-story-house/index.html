How to explore

Mouse or touch. Select Explore, then drag to turn. Use the zoom buttons, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose a room from the menu, or select a room in the drawing, to move closer. Choose Done exploring to scroll the page again.

Keyboard. Focus the drawing and press Enter. Arrow keys turn; Shift with arrow keys pans. Plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Escape ends exploring. Use the layer buttons or slider to reveal the house.

The finished house: a one-story L-shaped house, a large wing to the north-west joined to the main house. The roof is assumed (the set shows none). Choose Explore to turn the model or go to a room.

How the model was made

Modeled from the first floor plan, the ceiling plan and the interior elevations. Walls, openings and the printed opening widths come from the plan; the wall height comes from an interior elevation. The sheets show no roof and name no rooms, so the roof form and room names are listed below as assumptions.

Roof. The set has no roof plan or exterior elevations, so a simple hipped roof over the whole footprint is assumed.

North-west wing. A large open room and a smaller room, with a bath and laundry beside them, make up the wing. It joins the main house through a hall.

Openings. Two 8 foot windows and the entry door face south, a 5 foot 6 inch sliding glass door and two windows face north, and the openings use the widths printed on the plan.

Assumptions (not printed on the plans)

  • Scale: the sheet states 3/8 inch to the foot, but the drawing measures 28.58 points to the foot (the printed 8 foot, 6 foot and 5 foot 6 inch openings fit that scale), so lengths are taken at that scale.
  • Room names are not printed on the plan. The sheets number the rooms (1 to 9) on the elevation tags; names appear only where fixtures show the use, and room areas are traced inside the wall faces.
  • Wall height: 7 feet 11 1/2 inches, the one wall height printed (interior elevation 1-d), applied to every wall. The ceiling plan prints ceiling heights of 7 feet 2 inches to 7 feet 9 inches in places; room labels are not affected.
  • Roof: the set has no roof plan or exterior elevations. A hipped roof over the whole footprint is assumed, pitch 4 in 12 with 18 inch overhangs.
  • The floor is drawn 1 foot above grade: the floor-to-grade height is not printed, and no steps are drawn.
  • Window and door sizes are taken from the printed opening widths where the plan gives them; sills at 3 feet and heads at 6 feet 8 inches are assumed, as are all door heights.
  • The two stacked boxes on the east wall of the front room are drawn as hollow wall outlines, as the plan shows them; their use is not printed.
  • Kitchen, bath and laundry fixtures are placed from the plan symbols; their heights are assumed.
