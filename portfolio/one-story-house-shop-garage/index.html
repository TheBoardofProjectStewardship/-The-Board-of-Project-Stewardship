How to explore

Mouse or touch. Select Explore, then drag to turn. Use the zoom buttons, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose a room from the menu, or select a room in the drawing, to move closer. Choose Done exploring to scroll the page again.

Keyboard. Focus the drawing and press Enter. Arrow keys turn; Shift with arrow keys pans. Plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Escape ends exploring. Use the layer buttons or slider to reveal the house.

Top view looks straight down at the plan. Zooming in lifts the roof and reveals room names. Reset view returns to the selected layer.

The finished house: a one-story home with an attached garage and shop. Choose Explore to turn the model or go to a room.

How the model was made

Modeled from a one-page layout plan. Walls, openings, room names, areas and ceiling heights come from the plan. The roof, window sills and heads, and door heights are not drawn on the plan and are shown as labeled assumptions.

Roof. The layout does not draw the roof, so this model shows a simple low-slope roof over each ceiling height. The living and dining room rises above the rest.

Garage and shop. The garage, 2,511 SF as printed, wraps around a 406 SF shop and a small bath. Its overhead doors face the apron.

Openings. Windows, doors and the two bays are placed where the layout leaves gaps in the walls. Sill and head heights are typical values, not printed.

Room layout. Room names, areas and ceiling heights are the ones printed on the layout. Select a room to fly in.

Slab. A one-story house on a slab: the floor, the covered patio, the entry porch and the apron.
