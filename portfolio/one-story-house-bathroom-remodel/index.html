How to explore

Mouse or touch. Select Explore, then drag to turn. Use the zoom buttons, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose a room from the menu, or select a room in the drawing, to move closer. Choose Done exploring to scroll the page again.

Keyboard. Focus the drawing and press Enter. Arrow keys turn; Shift with arrow keys pans. Plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Escape ends exploring. Use the layer buttons or slider to reveal the house.

The finished house: a one-story house with the attached garage on the east end and a patio behind the dining room. The roof is assumed (the set shows none). Choose Explore to turn the model or go to a room.

How the model was made

Modeled from the proposed main floor plan. Walls, openings and room names come from the plan; the wall height is not printed. The sheets show no roof, so the roof form and heights are listed below as assumptions.

Roof. The set has no roof plan or exterior elevations, so a simple hipped roof over the house and garage is assumed.

Baths. Two baths sit side by side at the north of the bedroom wing. Each has a tub and shower, a toilet and a vanity, and opens to the hall.

Openings. The entry door and porch face south beside the living room window, and the dining room opens to the patio through a sliding glass door on the north.

Assumptions (not printed on the plans)

  • Scale: the sheet states 1/4 inch to the foot (18 points per foot) and the printed 5 ft and 6 ft 10 in bath widths, the 8 ft bath depth, the 2 ft 6 in doors and the 4 ft 8 in window all measure within about 2 percent of that scale, so lengths are taken at 18 points per foot.
  • Wall height: 8 feet, not printed anywhere on the set, applied to every wall. Wall thickness is the thickness drawn on the plan.
  • Roof: the set has no roof plan or exterior elevations. A hipped roof over the house and garage is assumed, pitch 4 in 12 with 18 inch overhangs.
  • The floor is drawn 1 foot above grade: the floor-to-grade height is not printed, and no steps are drawn. The patio and porch slabs are drawn just below the floor.
  • Window and door sizes are traced from the openings on the plan; only the bath window (4 ft 8 in), the bath doors (2 ft 6 in) and the bath dimensions are printed. Sills at 3 feet and heads at 6 feet 8 inches are assumed, as are all door heights and the garage door height.
  • The sheet prints room names for the bedrooms, baths, kitchen, dining, living room, garage, porch and patio. The small room at the west end of the hall and the hall itself are not named.
  • The living room, kitchen and dining room are one open space. The lines between them are placed at the counter ends and the garage wall.
  • Bath, kitchen and counter fixtures are placed from the plan symbols; their heights are assumed. The refrigerator, range and counters in the kitchen are drawn as simple boxes.
  • The patio (15 by 15 feet) and porch (5 by 3 feet) are the concrete areas outlined on the plan.
