How to explore

Mouse or touch. Select Explore, then drag to turn. Use the zoom buttons, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose a room from the menu, or select a room in the drawing, to move closer. Choose Done exploring to scroll the page again.

Keyboard. Focus the drawing and press Enter. Arrow keys turn; Shift with arrow keys pans. Plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Escape ends exploring. Use the layer buttons or slider to reveal the house.

The finished house: a one-story, three-bedroom house under a hipped roof. The remodel works inside the existing walls. Choose Explore to turn the model or go to a room.

How the model was made

Modeled from the proposed main level plan, drawn to scale at 3/8 inch to the foot with printed dimensions, plus the framing plan and the new bath interior elevation. Walls, openings, new window sizes and room names come from the plan; the wall height comes from the bath elevation and the roof form from the framing plan's approximate roof layout. Values the sheets do not print are listed below as assumptions.

Roof. The framing plan draws one approximate hipped roof over the whole house. The remodel keeps it; the engineer notes that no roof should bear on the walls being removed.

Bedroom wing. Old closets in the bedroom wing come out. New walls make a bath between the front bedroom and the existing bath, with a 60 inch double vanity, a walk-in shower and the toilet in its own alcove, a walk-in closet behind it, new closets for two bedrooms and a linen closet off the hall.

Openings. New 36 by 60 inch egress windows in the front bedroom, a high 48 by 12 inch window over the new shower, a 72 by 24 inch window and a 60 by 35 inch window in the side bedroom, and 30 by 80 inch doors, two of them pocket doors into the new bath. Two old windows are filled in.

  • New bath, walk-in closet and three new closets
  • Kitchen, laundry, pantry and living room kept in place
  • Plan drawn to scale with printed dimensions

Assumptions (not printed on the plans)

  • Room areas are not printed on the plan; the labels show areas traced inside the wall faces, and the smallest rooms show their names only.
  • Wall and ceiling height: 7 feet 7 inches (91 inches), as printed on the new bath interior elevation; the same height is assumed in every room.
  • Roof: the framing plan draws an approximate hipped roof layout over the whole house. Pitch and overhang are not printed: assumed 6 in 12, as detail 8 on the framing sheets draws the existing rafters, with 18 inch overhangs.
  • The floor is drawn 1 foot 3 inches above grade over a crawl space; the floor-to-grade height is not printed, and entry steps are not drawn.
  • New window sizes are printed on the plan (width by height); their heads are set at 6 feet 8 inches, except the 48 by 12 inch shower window, whose head (6 feet 10 inches) is scaled from the bath elevation.
  • Existing window and door sizes are not printed: sills at 3 feet and heads at 6 feet 8 inches are assumed.
  • The existing fireplace is drawn as a solid mass with its opening toward the living room; its chimney is not drawn on the plans and is not modeled.
  • Two existing metal posts are marked on the concrete pad outside the kitchen. Their height is not printed (drawn 8 feet), and whatever they carry is not drawn, so nothing is modeled on top of them.
  • The heat pump outdoor unit beside the front bedroom is drawn as a 3 foot tall box over the dashed outline on the plan; its height is not printed.
  • Kitchen, laundry and bath fixtures are placed from the plan symbols; their heights are assumed.
