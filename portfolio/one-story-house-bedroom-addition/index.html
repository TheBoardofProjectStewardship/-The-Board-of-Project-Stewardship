How to explore

Mouse or touch. Select Explore, then drag to turn. Use the zoom buttons, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Choose a room from the menu, or select a room in the drawing, to move closer. Choose Done exploring to scroll the page again.

Keyboard. Focus the drawing and press Enter. Arrow keys turn; Shift with arrow keys pans. Plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Escape ends exploring. Use the layer buttons or slider to reveal the house.

The house after the work: a one-story home with a new bedroom filled in between its two west wings under a low hip, gables on the wings and a gabled main block. Seen from the southwest, where the addition is. Choose Explore to turn the model or go to a room.

How the model was made

Modeled from the approved plan set: the proposed floor plan, roof plan, elevations and section at 1/4 inch = 1 foot. Walls, openings, room names, window sizes, the roof shape and pitches and the printed plate, header and ridge heights come from those sheets. Anything the sheets do not show is an assumption, listed below.

Roof. The roof follows the proposed roof plan: 6:12 gables over the primary bedroom wing, the family room wing, the second bedroom and the main block, and a hip at 4 5/32:12 over the new bedroom that meets the 6:12 slopes in hips and valleys. The elevations print the ridge 18'-8 1/4" above the floor. Overhangs are scaled from the roof plan: 18 inches at the eaves, 2 feet at the addition and 3 inches at the rakes.

Openings. Window and door widths are traced from the floor plan and their heights read off the elevations, which print a 6'-11 3/8" window header. New windows carry their tags (5040, 3620, 5036: width and height in feet and inches); the rest are marked existing (EXST'G).

Room layout. The plan prints room names but no room areas except the 265 SF addition, so rooms show their names only. Select a room to fly in.

  • 1,895 SF main floor
  • 265 SF bedroom addition
  • Three bedrooms, two baths

Assumptions (not printed on the plans)

  • Walls are traced from the proposed floor plan at 1/4 inch = 1 foot and checked against the printed dimensions. The traced house is about 1,934 SF to the outside faces against the printed 1,895 SF main floor; the addition traces to 265.6 SF against the printed 265 SF.
  • Heights come from the proposed elevations: top plate 8'-1 1/8", window header 6'-11 3/8" and ridge 18'-8 1/4" above the floor. Ceilings are assumed 8'-0" and interior doors 6'-8".
  • The roof follows the proposed roof plan with the printed 6:12 and 4 5/32:12 pitches. Where the roof plan and the ridge height leave the eave height open, the roof planes are set to meet the printed ridge; the roof covering is shown as plain courses.
  • The ground is set from the elevations: about 2'-0" below the floor on the north and west sides, falling to about 2'-5" at the northeast corner and rising to about 1'-4" below the floor at the southeast corner. The crawl space under the floor is not shown.
  • The entry stoop, its steps down along the wall and its railing are scaled from the floor plan and the east and north elevations; the number of steps is assumed.
  • Window widths are traced from the plan; window heights are read off the elevations. The 3'-4" entry door is shown at the window header height.
  • The plans label two rooms Bath and three Bdrm; they are numbered here so the room picker can tell them apart.
  • Kitchen, bath, laundry and bedroom fixtures sit where the plan symbols are, scaled from the drawing. Their heights are assumed. Interior doors are shown as open gaps. Closet shelving, siding, the stone wainscot and trim are not modeled.
