## Local context

Know which jurisdiction covers your parcel — city boundaries and permitting paths matter. School-oriented family homes often prioritize durable, flexible space over showpiece volumes. Ask for Northshore-area addition references.

## Climate and permitting

PNW rain makes flashing at the old-to-new joint critical. Energy code applies to new conditioned space. Electrical, plumbing, and mechanical permits follow when systems expand. Build review timelines into expectations.

## Process guidance

Site/zoning check → structural design → permit set → weatherproof dry-in → interiors. Occupied builds need temporary protection plans. Exterior matching should be specified early.

## FAQ

**Addition vs finishing a basement?**  
Basements depend on height, moisture, and egress. Compare apples-to-apples with a professional — not social media alone.

**Will my taxes change?**  
Assessed value can change after permitted heated space is added. Ask your assessor for process info; contractors do not control tax outcomes.

**What contingency is prudent?**  
Site and structural surprises deserve a contingency line discussed before signing.

## Northshore lot and jurisdiction checks

Bothell’s boundaries and nearby jurisdictions mean two similar-looking streets can have different permit counters. Confirm your parcel’s permitting authority before schematic design goes too far. Setbacks, lot coverage, and height limits shape whether a side bump-out or vertical addition is even viable.

Drainage deserves early attention: new roofs concentrate water. Downspout terminations, grading away from foundations, and crawlspace moisture control protect both old and new structure through wet months.

## Matching old and new structure

Foundation type, floor framing depth, and roof pitches should be surveyed before aesthetic massing is locked. A common disappointment is a beautiful addition with a trip step at the threshold because floor systems were never aligned. Design out elevation mismatches deliberately.

## Occupied-site addition practices

If you remain home, demand temporary weather protection whenever exterior walls open, daily cleanup paths, and a communication cadence for shutoffs. Pets and kids need defined safe zones. Ask for a construction fence or barrier plan when open excavations exist.

## Putting it together for homeowners

For **Bothell additions**, the durable projects share a pattern: respect **jurisdiction boundary checks and drainage from new roofs**, put **floor elevation matching between old and new volumes** in writing, and keep **occupied-site excavation barriers and shutoff notices** on the critical path instead of the punch list.
