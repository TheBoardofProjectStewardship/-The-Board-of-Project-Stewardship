Write the article body in Markdown below the frontmatter.

Use relative links to directory pages such as [Kitchen remodelers](../kitchen.html) or [Home additions](../index.html).

Author attribution on the public page is always **Board of Project Stewardship Editorial**.

New photos: name the file `yyyy-mm-dd-<city>-<topic>-N.webp` (lowercase city and topic, N starting at 1). Do not rename a photo that is already published. Alt text is one sentence of 125 characters or fewer and names the topic and the city once. The generator turns that alt into the visible caption.
