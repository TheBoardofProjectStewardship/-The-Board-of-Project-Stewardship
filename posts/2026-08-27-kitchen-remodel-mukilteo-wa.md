## Coastal cooking and finish stress

Mukilteo kitchens near the water see more hardware corrosion risk and moisture loading when doors open to decks. Specify quality hinges and slides, and keep exterior door weatherseals in scope if the kitchen connects outdoors. Soft-close mechanisms still fail early if salt air and grit are ignored — ask about finish grades suited to coastal exposure.

View-oriented kitchens tempt huge window walls behind sinks. Beautiful, and cold if glazing and flashing details are weak. Coordinate window replacement with cabinet runs so reveals and backsplash heights are intentional.

## Work flow for ferry-adjacent logistics

Traffic patterns near the waterfront can affect delivery windows. Build flexibility into material lead times. Confirm where trucks turn around on hillside streets before the cabinet truck arrives.

## Design details that improve daily use

Deep drawers for pots often beat lower doors. Trash pullouts near the sink reduce cross-traffic. Specify outlet placement for small appliances you actually use. If you host often, plan landing space beside the range and fridge so guests do not stand in the cook’s primary path.
