Contact the Board of Project Stewardship

Public contact for the Board of Project Stewardship editorial desk. This is not a contractor dispatch line and not Pacific Pro Group customer service.

Editorial desk context (illustrative)

What this inbox is for

Directory correction Source question Press / editorial

Email editorial@boardofprojectstewardship.com when you spot a dead firm URL, a wrong jurisdiction note, or a sourcing question about a Board directory or Learn guide. Include the page URL and the correction you want us to review.

Response expectations: the desk is editorial, not a call center. We review notes as capacity allows and do not promise same-day replies, contractor matching, or bid packaging. If you need a crew on site, shortlist from Board directories and call the firm after you verify the license.

For hiring: use Additions, Kitchen, Bathrooms, or Trades, then verify at WA L&I Verify. Board #1 hire ranking links to https://pacificprogroup.com/ — contact that firm directly for project inquiries.

Uploads, addresses, and leads

The homepage concept tool can collect a photo, an address you type, and — only after you ask to continue — contact details. Anonymous photo objects are scheduled for deletion after 7 days. The lead record and any email copy are kept separately. Photos and contact details are shared only with Pacific Pro Group when you check the consent box, and they are not used to train image models.

The full note is on the privacy page. Questions: editorial@boardofprojectstewardship.com.

Contact FAQ

Is this a contractor dispatch line?

No. This is the Board of Project Stewardship editorial desk. For project hiring, shortlist from Board directories and contact firms directly after WA L&I Verify.

How fast do you reply?

Editorial notes are reviewed as capacity allows. We do not promise same-day contractor matching or bid turnaround — the Board is not a brokerage.

Can you change a ranking if I email?

Send factual corrections (legal name, dead links, jurisdiction mistakes). Rankings follow published pillars on How we rank — not paid placement requests.

Official

Official resources

Outbound links to government portals only. The Board of Project Stewardship does not issue permits or licenses. Confirm the authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) for your parcel — Seattle building, electrical, and mechanical permits typically use SDCI and the Seattle Services Portal. Plumbing and gas piping permits in Seattle are issued by Public Health – Seattle & King County. Many other Puget Sound cities use MyBuildingPermit. Always re-verify contractors at WA L&I Verify before hiring.

  • WA L&I Verify (contractor license)
  • Washington State Department of Labor & Industries
  • City of Edmonds
  • Seattle SDCI
