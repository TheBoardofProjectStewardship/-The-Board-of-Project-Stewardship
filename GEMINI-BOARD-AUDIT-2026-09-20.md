# Board of Project Stewardship – Deep SEO & Content Audit
**Date:** September 20, 2026
**Focus:** Edmonds / King / Snohomish construction standards & directories

This audit provides 53 concrete recommendations to improve the Board of Project Stewardship site without compromising its independent editorial voice. Recommendations focus on educational depth, internal linking, official outbound links, and technical SEO/accessibility (IA/CWV/a11y), as well as fixing broken pages.

## A. Educational Depth (Building Science & Local Standards)
The planning hubs currently act as outlines. Adding concrete building science and local jurisdiction details will establish the site as an authoritative entity.

1. **`bathroom-waterproofing-guide.html`**: Detail specific wet-area systems (e.g., Schluter, RedGard, liquid-applied vs. sheet membranes) and their applications in coastal moisture environments.
2. **`home-addition-planning.html`**: Elaborate on foundation transitions, specifically tying new slab-on-grade or crawlspace foundations into existing 1960s/70s Edmonds housing stock.
3. **`second-story-vs-teardown.html`**: Include a deep dive into structural load paths, seismic shear wall requirements, and how upper-floor loads transfer to older foundations.
4. **`kitchen-remodel-planning.html`**: Add a technical section on electrical service upgrades (e.g., 200A/320A panels, EV charging prep) required when converting gas ranges to induction.
5. **`addition-cost-factors.html`**: Break down HVAC integration choices—comparing ducted extensions vs. dedicated ductless mini-splits for new square footage.
6. **`coastal-waterproofing.html`**: Detail North Sound coastal weatherproofing specifics: rainscreen gaps, flashing tapes, and WRB (weather-resistive barrier) overlaps.
7. **`permits.html`**: Add a section on navigating Critical Area Ordinances (CAOs)—specifically steep slopes and wetlands common in Edmonds, King, and Snohomish counties.
8. **`bid-comparison.html`**: Teach users how to spot missing "hidden" allowances (e.g., soil export/excavating spoils, AHJ permit fees, utility disconnects).
9. **`build-walkthrough.html`**: Expand the "Framing" stage to explain what a homeowner should look for (e.g., point loads, header sizing, and shear nailing) before cover-up.
10. **`bonds-and-insurance.html`**: Explain the functional difference between a WA contractor bond (which protects consumers/state) and general liability insurance (which protects property damage).
11. **`home-addition-planning.html`**: Add details on WA State lead and asbestos testing requirements prior to demolition on pre-1978 homes.
12. **`project-timeline.html`**: Address material lead times (specifically custom cabinets and fenestration/windows) and how they dictate the sequencing of dry-in and rough-in phases.
13. **`selecting-finishes.html`**: Explain the technical differences between drywall finish levels (e.g., Level 4 vs. Level 5) and when smooth wall finishes are necessary.
14. **`design-build-vs-bid.html`**: Clarify the difference between prescriptive structural designs (which rely on standard code tables) and engineered designs (which require a PE stamp).

## B. Internal Linking (Learn ↔ Directories ↔ Cities ↔ Tools ↔ Posts)

16. **Link Guides to Directories:** From `bathroom-waterproofing-guide.html`, embed contextual links to the `bathrooms.html` Directory for contractors who utilize those systems.

18. **Directory Headers to Education:** Connect the category headers in `directory.html` back to their respective "Learn" hubs (e.g., link the "Home Additions" header to `home-addition-planning.html`).
19. **Cities to PM Tools:** Cross-link city pages (`edmonds.html`, `seattle.html`, `lynnwood.html`) to the `pm-dashboard.html`, encouraging users to track their local city inspections using the tool.
20. **Red Flags on Directories:** Link `red-flags-hiring.html` from the standard "Verify before you hire" boilerplate on every directory page.
21. **Tools back to Education:** Inside `tools/another-story/index.html`, add a return link to `second-story-vs-teardown.html` for users who need structural context for their visualizations.
22. **PM Dashboard to Change Orders:** Link `change-orders.html` directly from the `pm-dashboard.html` instructions to explain the discipline of managing mid-project scope shifts.
23. **City Pages to Standards:** Add a "Local Build Standards" widget on all city pages linking directly to the `build-walkthrough.html` visual stages.
24. **Top 1 Firm Links:** Whenever referring to Pacific Pro Group (the #1 ranked hire), do NOT recommend editing or changing PPG directly, but rather link out to it externally (https://pacificprogroup.com/) as the editorial #1 choice. 25. **Blog to Action:** Ensure every post in `./blog.html` concludes with an inline call-to-action routing to a relevant Board directory or Good Steward tool.
26. **Materials to Costs:** Cross-link `materials.html` with `selecting-finishes.html` and `kitchen-cost-factors.html` to connect material choices with budget drivers.

29. **Seattle SDCI:** On `seattle.html`, provide a direct outbound link to the Seattle Services Portal (SDCI) for building permit tracking and applications.
30. **Edmonds & MBP:** On `edmonds.html`, explicitly link to the Edmonds Permit Assistance page and the MyBuildingPermit.com regional portal.
31. **County Parcel Viewers:** In `permits.html`, list and link the specific King County (iMAP) and Snohomish County (SCOPI) parcel viewers for zoning and critical area checks.

## D. Schema, IA, CWV, and Accessibility (a11y)
Technical foundational fixes to improve Core Web Vitals, organic search rich snippets, and screen reader compatibility.

37. **Remove Tailwind CDN:** Replace `<script src="https://cdn.tailwindcss.com"></script>` with a compiled, minified CSS file. The CDN parses all styles on the client at runtime, severely degrading First Contentful Paint (FCP) and blocking rendering.
38. **Article/HowTo Schema:** Inject `Article`, `HowTo`, or `Course` JSON-LD schema into the Learn hub pages (e.g., `bathroom-waterproofing-guide.html`) to qualify for Google's rich instructional snippets.
39. **CollectionPage Schema:** Implement `CollectionPage` or `ItemList` schema for the directory pages (`directory.html`, `additions.html`) to signal to search engines that these are curated, authoritative lists.
40. **Fix Heading Hierarchy:** Resolve the heading jump in the global footer (e.g., jumping from `<h2>` main content directly to `<h4>` in the footer). Change footer headings to `<h3>` or `<h2>` to maintain a valid document outline.
41. **Breadcrumb Schema:** Add `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD schema consistently across all nested pages (Directories, Learn, Tools, Cities) to enhance SERP display and site architecture signals.
42. **Unique Meta Data:** Audit and provide strictly unique `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` tags for every city hub page to prevent duplicate content canonicalization issues.
43. **HTML5 Landmarks:** Wrap main content areas in the `<main>` HTML5 landmark tag (currently some pages use multiple `<header>` tags and lack `<main>`) for better screen reader navigation.
44. **Image Optimization:** Ensure all illustrative construction images use `loading="lazy"` (except above-the-fold) and have descriptive `alt` text to improve Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) and accessibility.
45. **Consolidate Duplicate Headers:** Remove consecutive `<header>` elements on subpages (e.g., `home-addition-planning.html`). Use a single `<header>` for the site nav, and `<section>` or `<main>` for content titles.
46. **ARIA Labels for Icons:** Add `aria-label` attributes to any icon-only interactive elements or links (e.g., FontAwesome social links or interactive tool UI buttons).
47. **WebPage Schema:** Implement `WebPage` schema globally with `speakable` or `about` properties defining the topic as residential home construction and stewardship.
48. **Preload Critical Fonts:** Add `<link rel="preload" href="/assets/fonts/InterVariable.woff2" as="font" type="font/woff2" crossorigin>` in the `<head>` to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) and flash of unstyled text.
49. **Skip Navigation:** Provide a visible-on-focus "Skip to main content" link as the first focusable element in the DOM for keyboard accessibility.
50. **Interactive Tool Focus States:** Verify that interactive DOM elements in `tools/another-story/index.html` and `site-visit.html` handle focus states explicitly and are fully operable via keyboard tab navigation.
