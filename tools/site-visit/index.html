PRO TIP:

Let the client do 80% of the talking during the initial walkthrough. Focus on listening to their pain points with the current space.

🗣 Phase 3: Core Discovery Questions

Ask in this exact order – take verbatim notes.

🎯 Vision & Use

1. What is the #1 reason you’re doing this addition?

2. How will the space actually be used day-to-day?

3. Any must-have features?

💰 Budget Reality Check

4. What budget range feels comfortable right now?

5. Have you gotten any other bids or ballpark numbers already?

6. Do you have financing pre-approved or are you paying cash?

7. Any hard budget cap we should know about to avoid surprises later?

🎨 Style & Inspiration

8. What overall style are you drawn to?

9. Show 3 recent coastal addition photos → "Which feels most like you?"

10. Do you have a Pinterest board, Houzz ideas, or saved photos?

📐 Client's Existing Plans

11. Sketches, drawings, or ideas from others?

12. Worked with an architect before?

13. Specific layouts mapped out?

📅 Timeline & Life

14. When do you need this finished by?

15. Will you live in the house during construction?

🌊 Site & Coastal Specifics (Edmonds WA)

16. Any known issues with foundation, drainage, or siding?

17. Have you had moisture/mold problems on this side of the house?

18. HOA rules, view corridors, or neighbor concerns?

19. Wetlands, shorelines, flood zones, steep slopes? (Critical Area check)

20. Do you know if the property is on septic or city sewer?

⚡ Utilities & MEP Systems

Electrical Panel Size?

Gas Service on Property?

Primary HVAC System?

Water Heater Type?
