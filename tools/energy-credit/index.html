WA window energy credit estimator

Worksheet for Edmonds / King & Snohomish homeowners comparing published federal §25C window limits with common utility rebate schedules. Numbers below are from linked public pages as of the Board’s 2026-09-20 review.

For §25C envelope components, IRS instructions generally exclude onsite prep/install labor from the component cost. Enter only amounts you believe are eligible — this tool does not decide eligibility.

Sources (public)

  • IRS — Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit / Form 5695: windows & skylights 30% with $600 combined cap; annual envelope-style limit $1,200 (with separate heat-pump rules). Instructions for Form 5695; IRS 25C FAQ.
  • IRS OBBB FAQs — §25C not allowed for property placed in service after December 31, 2025. IRS OBBB energy credit FAQs.
  • PSE windows rebate page (purchase/install through 12/31/2026 per PSE page; apply within 60 days): standard published amounts include $50 or $100 per qualifying window by U-factor / existing type, with published program caps; Efficiency Boost amounts higher. pse.com/en/rebates/windows-rebates.
  • Snohomish PUD weatherization: published $100 per replaced single-pane window; $50 for qualifying double-pane metal-frame replacements; U-0.30 or lower; PUD-registered contractor; electric heat. snopud.com weatherization rebates.
