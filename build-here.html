Good Steward Tools · Feasibility

What can I build here?

Type an address in King or Snohomish County. We read the public parcel, zoning and hazard maps, apply the code sections we have checked, and lay out a free first-pass report for an addition, an ADU, a teardown or a remodel. You can print it or save it as a PDF.

Covered today: City of Seattle (Neighborhood Residential zones), City of Edmonds (LDR zones), unincorporated King County and unincorporated Snohomish County. Other cities get the parcel facts and a note to check with the city.

1. Find the lot

The address goes to the US Census geocoder and to county and city map services. We do not store it.

2. Check the numbers

Public records can be stale or missing. Fix anything that looks wrong; the report uses what you see here.

3. Your feasibility snapshot

Budget and resale calculator

Plain math on your own numbers. The starting values are examples only, not market data or a quote. Replace every one.

Interest is simple interest on the full loan for every month, which overstates carry on a loan that draws down over time. Taxes, insurance and utilities are not included; add them to soft costs if you want them counted.

Want a second look? (optional)

Send the Board editors your address and question. This opens your own email app addressed to editorial@boardofprojectstewardship.com, the same inbox our other forms use. We do not sell leads.

Read this before you rely on it

This page gives a planning estimate. It is not a legal, zoning or permit determination, a survey, or professional advice. Public maps can be out of date, and codes change. Confirm every number with the city or county and with a licensed architect, engineer, surveyor or contractor before you buy, design or build. Rules shown were last checked October 2026 against the official code pages linked in each report.
