Add a source-backed number

Use this when an automatic layer is blank. Type a figure you read on an assessor card, a survey, a permit plan, or a listing page. This tool does not fetch listing sites. An aerial photo or an unscaled picture needs a low and a high, not one precise number.

    Read this before you rely on it

    This tool gives a planning estimate. It is not a legal, zoning or permit determination, a survey, or professional advice. Public maps can be out of date, and codes change. Confirm every number with the city or county and with a licensed architect, engineer, surveyor or contractor before you buy, design or build.
