Design it in 3D

Your lot from What can I build here?, with the setback envelope and height limit drawn in. Add boxes for a house, addition, ADU or shop, stack floors, and check them against the caps. For walls, doors and rooms, continue in Pascal Editor below.

Selected: W ft · D ft · Floors · Turn deg

Drag a box to move it. Drag empty ground to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Floors are 10 ft each. Diagram only, not a survey or a design.

Pascal Editor is an open-source (MIT) 3D building editor from Pascal Group: walls, slabs, roofs, doors, windows and zones, rendered with WebGPU, with AI agents that can build from a prompt. It runs best in a current Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Copy the brief below, open the editor, and paste it into its AI chat, or draw the lot by hand with these numbers.

Open Pascal Editor in a new tab

Pascal Editor is made by Pascal Group Inc. and is open source on GitHub under the MIT license. The Board is not affiliated with Pascal. What you draw there stays in that app.

CivilCraft is a free, open-source (Apache-2.0) civil and site design app from the CadCraft suite. It builds a ground surface from survey points, then lets you grade a building pad, lay a driveway, see cut and fill, and check drainage. It runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded. Download your lot as a point file below, open CivilCraft, and drop the file in.

Open CivilCraft in a new tab 10-minute guide

The point file has your lot corners and the setback envelope at elevation 100 ft, as a flat stand-in. Real grades need a survey: replace them with your surveyor's points before you trust any cut, fill or slope number.

CivilCraft is made by CadCraft and is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. The Board is not affiliated with CadCraft.
